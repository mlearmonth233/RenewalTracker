How alerts work

  • Each item has reminder stages (e.g. 30,7,1 days before). An alert is raised once per stage, plus on the day and when overdue.
  • The server checks automatically every few hours. You can also trigger a check from the dashboard.
  • Passports default to reminders at 270, 180 and 90 days because many countries require six months of remaining validity.
  • Export your renewals to your calendar app with the .ics button on the Items page.
